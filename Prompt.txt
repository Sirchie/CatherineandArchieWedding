Build a single-file HTML wedding e-invitation website (HTML + CSS + minimal
vanilla JS inline, no external dependencies). Mobile-first, since most guests
will open it on a phone from a messaging app link.

DESIGN
- Palette: rustic orange as the primary accent (terracotta/burnt orange,
  roughly #C1571F–#D2691E), on a warm off-white/cream background (#FAF6F0),
  with muted sage or warm brown as a secondary accent.
- Style: simple but elegant — generous white space, a refined serif for
  headings (Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display) paired with a clean sans
  for body text, subtle letter-spacing, thin divider rules, small botanical
  or floral line accents. No heavy textures, no clutter, no busy animation.
- Gentle fade-in on scroll and a soft hover state on buttons — nothing more.

SECTIONS (in order)
1. Hero — couple's names, wedding date, venue city, and a live countdown timer.
2. Our Story — short narrative plus an optional photo.
3. Event Details — ceremony and reception each with date, time, venue name,
   full address, and a "Get Directions" link that opens Google Maps.
4. Schedule / Order of the Day — simple vertical timeline.
5. Dress Code — attire guidance with a small colour-swatch strip.
6. Entourage / Wedding Party — grouped names by role.
7. Gift Guide — a short, tasteful note (e.g. monetary gifts preferred).
8. RSVP — form with name, number of guests, attending yes/no, meal choice,
   and a message field. Validate client-side; on submit show a thank-you
   state. Leave a clearly commented placeholder where a Google Form or
   Formspree endpoint can be dropped in.
9. FAQ — collapsible accordion.
10. Footer — hashtag, contact number, and a thank-you line.

TECHNICAL
- Responsive from 320px up; readable type at every size.
- Use clearly marked placeholder content (NAMES, DATE, VENUE, PHOTO) so
  every detail is easy to swap out.
- Semantic HTML, alt text on images, sufficient colour contrast.
- Include a favicon and Open Graph tags so the link previews nicely when
  shared on Messenger or Viber.
